Delete your Crachoir account

You can delete your Crachoir account and its data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

From the app

  1. Open Crachoir and sign in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete permanently.
  4. Sign in again when asked, to confirm it's you.

An internet connection is required.

By email

If you can no longer use the app, email crachoirapp@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and ask for your account to be deleted.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and sign-in information).
  • Your tastings and tasting notes.
  • Your cellar entries.
  • Your photos.

Your data is removed from our systems and from our service providers within a reasonable period. See the Privacy Policy for details.
