Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Crachoir ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles your information when you use the Crachoir application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at crachoirapp@gmail.com.

1. Who We Are

Crachoir is located in Montréal, Québec, Canada. We operate the Crachoir wine journaling and cellar management application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the App's features:

Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and, where applicable, your name and profile photo as provided by your authentication provider (Google). If you use email/password sign-in, we collect your email address.

User-generated content: Wine tasting notes, cellar entries, and other content you create within the App, including any photos you upload to illustrate your wines or cellar entries.

Technical information: Your device locale (language setting) is used to display the App in the correct language. No analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers are collected.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store, synchronize, and display your wine data and photos across your devices.
  • Authenticate you when you sign in.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the App.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

To provide the App's functionality, we share your information with the following processors:

Firebase Authentication (Google LLC): Handles account creation and sign-in. Your email address and, if you use Google Sign-In, your Google account identifiers are processed by Firebase. Privacy policy

Firebase Storage (Google LLC): Stores photos you upload to the App. Photos are associated with your account and stored on Google's infrastructure. Privacy policy

Couchbase Capella (Couchbase, Inc.): Provides cloud synchronization of your wine tasting data and cellar entries. Your data is stored on Couchbase's servers to enable sync across your devices. Privacy policy

Ko-fi (Ko-fi Labs Limited): The App contains a link to our Ko-fi page for voluntary tips. If you visit Ko-fi and choose to make a payment, that transaction is processed entirely by Ko-fi and Stripe under Ko-fi's own terms. We do not receive, store, or process any payment information. Privacy policy

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at crachoirapp@gmail.com. Upon deletion, your data will be removed from our systems and our third-party processors within a reasonable period.

6. Your Rights

Under PIPEDA and Quebec Law 25, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Withdraw consent to our collection and use of your data (which will require account closure).
  • Be informed of any breach of your personal information.
  • File a complaint with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec (CAI) if you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at crachoirapp@gmail.com.

7. Children

The App is not directed at individuals under the age of 14. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

8. International Data Transfers

Our third-party processors (Google, Couchbase) may store and process your data on servers located outside Quebec and Canada. By using the App, you consent to the transfer of your information to these servers, which may be subject to different data protection laws than those in your jurisdiction.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this document will be revised. Your continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you.
