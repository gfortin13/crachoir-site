Your personal wine journal & cellar manager

Crachoir is a free, local-first app for wine lovers. Record tasting notes, manage your cellar, and keep track of every bottle — offline by default, with optional cloud sync across your devices.

We do not sell products, subscriptions, or digital goods. Our Stripe account exists solely through Ko-fi, a Stripe Connect partner platform, which I use to accept voluntary tips from users who want to support the app's development. Tips are optional and one-time — no goods or services are exchanged in return.
