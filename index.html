Your personal wine journal & cellar manager

Crachoir is a free, local-first app for wine lovers. Record tasting notes, manage your cellar, and keep track of every bottle — offline by default, with optional cloud sync across your devices.

We do not sell products, subscriptions, or digital goods. Our Stripe account exists solely through Ko-fi, a Stripe Connect partner platform, which I use to accept voluntary tips from users who want to support the app's development. These are one-time, voluntary donations — no goods or services are exchanged in return.

There is no storefront, no checkout flow, and no recurring billing. All payment processing happens through Ko-fi's platform directly. My app does not handle or store any payment information.

Built for privacy: no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Your tasting data stays on your device unless you choose to sync it. Crachoir does not sell or share your information with third parties beyond the service providers needed to run the app.
